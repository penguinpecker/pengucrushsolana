Description
Join Kip, Rook and Juno for a colourful match-3 adventure across 100 free levels and ten islands. Chain combos, rescue chicks and unleash powerful boosters, including Polar Blast and Aurora Sweep. Enjoy offline play, original music and daily quests. Connect your Solana wallet for optional booster packs or submit a completed score to the leaderboard for 0.01 SOL plus the network fee. Progress is saved on your device. No ads, subscriptions, token rewards or cash prizes.
