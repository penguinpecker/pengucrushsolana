GAME AND PAYMENT BEHAVIOUR
All 100 levels remain free; individual levels can be played offline. Continuing
after a completed level asks the Seeker wallet to approve the activity Memo
transaction and network fee. Leaderboard in the bottom navigation opens overall
and per-level rankings. Each completed level also offers an optional 0.01 SOL
score submission, plus the network fee.
Only a wallet's best submitted score per level contributes to its overall total.
There are no prizes, payouts, subscriptions, token rewards or paid loot boxes.

Fixed packs in Pack:
3 hammers + 3 shuffles: 0.005 SOL
10 hammers + 10 shuffles: 0.015 SOL
25 hammers + 25 shuffles: 0.03 SOL
2 Polar Blasts + 2 Aurora Sweeps: 0.01 SOL
6 Polar Blasts + 6 Aurora Sweeps: 0.025 SOL

Polar Blast clears a 5x5 area. Aurora Sweep clears a chosen piece colour across
the board. Both trigger specials, chip obstacles and use no moves. Select a
target to preview, then tap Use or Cancel. One of each comes with the update;
more cost 180/220 earned shells each or the fixed SOL packs above.
All prices exclude the wallet-displayed network fee. Wallet sign-in itself does
not transfer SOL. Purchases use native Mobile Wallet Adapter to open the wallet.
Exact finalized payments are verified by the server before crediting the order.
Use Pack > Check status to recover an interrupted wallet return.
Paid consumables stay on the purchasing installation. Deleting a save, clearing
storage or uninstalling removes unused supplies. Receipt IDs prevent duplicate
credit on the same installation. There is no cloud save or cross-device restore.
